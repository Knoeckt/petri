Petri / Orbit

Milestone 0 mockup. One set of mechanics, two skins. Timings are compressed about 6× (a 20 s cycle here is 2 min in the real game). Ads are a 3 s fake with a 20 s cooldown.

  • Strains are characters. Every catalog entry has a look and a temperament. Most are fuzzy guys. Some bite.
  • Dangerous strains (⚠ in the catalog) eat neighbouring colonies while the cycle runs. Tap one to quarantine it. Nibbler eats once; Gulp eats one every five seconds; The Wipe eats one every 2.5 s until the dish is empty. Antibiotic wash research contains them automatically, which is what protects you offline.
  • Rarity comes from the dish level only (plus luck). Level 1 is all common; level 10 is 85 / 12 / 3; level 20 adds epics; 35 adds legendaries.
  • Long timers have an ad. Finish research, a brew or a splice; warp an hour; boost; double what came in while you were away. The dish itself has no skip: the ring shows the cycle and a Harvest button appears when it is done.
  • Side buttons. Quests holds the odd jobs (the Clinic is story only now, with a "coming up" list so you can hunt ahead). Tickets is the pipette mini-game: three a day, stop the drop near the middle for a rarer artifact. Decor places up to three artifacts in the enclosure; only placed ones count. Shop sells a ticket, a mystery pebble and a paid boost.
  • Brewery button opens the Copper Kettle: pick a medicine from the rack, press Brew, tap the fire to hurry it. Splicer button opens the Splice-o-matic once Doc Ferro hands it over. Tap a tube to load a strain, pull the lever, tap the chamber to collect, tap a jar to seed it. Any two strains can go in; only five pairs react. Notes is Doc Ferro's notebook.
  • Upgrades are one-time, in four tiers of five. Buy enough in a tier to open the next. Dish level stays repeatable. The Upgrades tab gets a red dot when you can afford one.
  • Scale up needs the chapter finished and 10 of 13 found. Everything ×5, cycle ×1.5, catalog and research kept.
